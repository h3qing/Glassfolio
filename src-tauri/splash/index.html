Your portfolio is encrypted on this Mac

Glassfolio just made a key that locks your data. It's kept in your Mac's Keychain and never leaves this computer.

If the Keychain is ever lost (a new Mac, a reset, a failed disk), a copy of this key, your recovery key, is the only way back in. Nobody can recover it for you, including the people who make Glassfolio.

Save your recovery key

Write it down or print it, and keep it somewhere safe and offline. A password manager works too.

This key unlocks your Glassfolio data if your Mac's Keychain is lost. Keep this sheet private and offline.
